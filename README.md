# TestGorilla Study Notes

A free, complete study guide for TestGorilla hiring assessments: how an assessment is put together, what you will see on screen, a clear method for the six cognitive tests candidates meet most often, 24 original practice items with full explanations, how percentile scoring works, and study plans for a week or a weekend. TestGorilla is a platform, not a single test, so this guide starts by showing you how to work out what your own assessment contains. Facts about TestGorilla come only from TestGorilla's own pages and help centres, each one [cited](docs/sources.md).

Want to see where you stand first? Take the [free timed TestGorilla-style practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/testgorilla?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=t7cs68&utm_content=testgorilla), or the shorter [5-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md) in this repo.

## Start here

1. **Check your invitation.** It lists the tests in your assessment and how long you will have. Read [how a TestGorilla assessment works](docs/test-overview.md) to make sense of it.
2. **Find your weak sections.** Take the [5-minute diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md), at least the parts that match your tests. It has one item for each test.
3. **Fix the weakest section first.** Open its guide below, learn the method and the traps, then do its 3 practice items.
4. **Follow a plan.** Use the [7-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#7-day-plan), or the [2-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if your deadline is close.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| What it is | An online hiring assessment that each employer builds on the TestGorilla platform |
| Tests per assessment | Up to 5, chosen by the employer from TestGorilla's library |
| Other parts | Custom questions (essay, multiple choice, file upload, video), AI interviews, qualifying questions, personality tests |
| Typical length | About 60 to 90 minutes, per TestGorilla. Your welcome screen gives the real estimate |
| Cognitive tests | 17 in the library, each listed at 9 to 13 minutes |
| Questions per test | Not published on TestGorilla's test pages |
| Timer | Every test is timed, per test and/or per question. It keeps running if you leave |
| Going back | Not possible. Skipped questions count as wrong |
| Wrong answers | No extra penalty, so always answer |
| Allowed | Pen, paper and a calculator. No AI tools or outside help |
| Monitoring | Full-screen tracking, and webcam snapshots unless the employer turns them off |
| Scoring | Percent correct and a percentile against other candidates. No pass mark: each employer sets its own bar |
| Retakes | One attempt per invitation. A second try is up to the employer |

Sources: TestGorilla's [candidate guide](https://candidates.testgorilla.com/hc/en-us/articles/19092940874395-Comprehensive-guide-to-taking-your-assessment), [candidate FAQ](https://candidates.testgorilla.com/hc/en-us/articles/30851898009115-Common-questions-about-assessments-and-tests), [test library](https://www.testgorilla.com/test-library/cognitive-ability-tests/) and [percentile scoring guide](https://support.testgorilla.com/hc/en-us/articles/30054006666011-Understanding-percentile-scoring), checked 24 November 2026.

## The six tests at a glance

| Test | Listed time | Level | You mostly | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Numerical reasoning | 10 min | Intermediate | Calculate, estimate and read tables | [Numerical](docs/sections/numerical-reasoning.md) |
| Verbal reasoning | 10 min | Intermediate | Match word relationships and judge conclusions from text | [Verbal](docs/sections/verbal-reasoning.md) |
| Critical thinking | 12 min | Advanced | Solve syllogisms and arrangements, spot assumptions and flaws | [Critical thinking](docs/sections/critical-thinking.md) |
| Attention to detail (textual) | 12 min (page also says 10) | Intermediate | Compare names, numbers and records for small differences | [Attention to detail](docs/sections/attention-to-detail.md) |
| Spatial reasoning | 10 min | Intermediate | Rotate, fold and track shapes in your head | [Spatial](docs/sections/spatial-reasoning.md) |
| Problem solving | 9 min | Intermediate | Build schedules and rank tasks by rules | [Problem solving](docs/sections/problem-solving.md) |

Every test guide has the same parts: what it measures, how the questions look, a step-by-step method, the traps that cost points, timing advice, 3 original practice items with explanations, and a score table.

## How to use this repo

- **Read on GitHub.** Every page has links to the previous page, the next page and back to these contents, so you can read it start to finish like a book.
- **Study only what you will sit.** Your assessment has up to five tests. Skip the guides for tests that are not on your invitation.
- **Test yourself honestly.** Answers are hidden under "Answers and explanations". Commit to an answer before you open them, just as the real test will not let you go back.
- **Read every explanation,** including for items you got right. Most explanations say why the nearest wrong answer is wrong, which is where the learning is.
- **Keep a miss log.** For each miss, write the rule you broke. The [study plans](docs/study-plans.md#how-to-review-a-missed-item) show how.
- **Practise timed.** The notes teach the methods. A timed run, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/testgorilla?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=t7cs68&utm_content=testgorilla), teaches the pace.
- **Clone or fork it** if you want to annotate your own copy. The structure also works as a GitBook space: see [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md).

## About the practice items

All 24 practice items (18 in the six test guides and 6 in the diagnostic) are original, written for these notes. None are taken from TestGorilla, from any employer's assessment or from any prep site. They follow the skills TestGorilla lists for each test, so the methods you learn here transfer to the real thing. The spatial items are drawn as text grids; the real spatial test uses images.
