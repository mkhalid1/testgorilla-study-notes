# How a TestGorilla assessment works

TestGorilla is not one test. It is a hiring platform, and each employer builds its own **assessment** by choosing tests from TestGorilla's library and adding its own questions. Two candidates applying to two different companies can sit very different TestGorilla assessments. This page explains how an assessment is put together, so you can work out what yours is likely to contain.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| What it is | An online hiring assessment, built by the employer on the TestGorilla platform |
| Tests per assessment | Up to 5 skills tests |
| Custom questions | Employers can add their own. TestGorilla gives limits of up to 20, depending on the employer's plan |
| Typical length | TestGorilla says most assessments take about 60 to 90 minutes |
| Cognitive tests in the library | 17, each listed at 9 to 13 minutes |
| Timer | Every test is timed. A timer shows per test and/or per question |
| Going back | Not possible. Once you submit or skip a question, it is gone |
| Skipped questions | Count as wrong. TestGorilla says there is no penalty for a wrong answer, so guess |
| Allowed | Pen, paper and a calculator. AI tools and outside help are not allowed |
| Retakes | One attempt per invitation. A second try is up to the employer |
| Languages | Set by the employer. TestGorilla offers the candidate experience in 12 languages |
| Results | Sent to the employer. You see them only if the employer chooses to share them |

Sources: TestGorilla's [candidate guide](https://candidates.testgorilla.com/hc/en-us/articles/19092940874395-Comprehensive-guide-to-taking-your-assessment), [candidate FAQ](https://candidates.testgorilla.com/hc/en-us/articles/30851898009115-Common-questions-about-assessments-and-tests), [finishing guide](https://candidates.testgorilla.com/hc/en-us/articles/42091124058011-Finishing-your-assessment-and-what-happens-next) and [test library](https://www.testgorilla.com/test-library/cognitive-ability-tests/), checked 24 November 2026. Full list on the [sources page](sources.md).

## Tests, custom questions and assessments

TestGorilla uses two words carefully:

- A **test** is a set of questions on one skill, such as Numerical Reasoning or Microsoft Excel. Questions are drawn from a question bank.
- An **assessment** is the whole package the employer sends you: up to five tests plus any custom questions.

TestGorilla's candidate guide says each assessment must contain at least one test or one custom question, and can include up to five tests and up to 20 custom questions. Its employer help pages give slightly different custom-question limits by plan (5, 10 or 20), so the exact number depends on the employer's account. The welcome screen of your assessment tells you how many timed tests and questions you will get, and roughly how long it will take.

## The cognitive tests in the library

TestGorilla's library has a cognitive ability category with 17 tests. These are the ones this guide covers, with the duration and level TestGorilla lists for each:

| Test | Listed time | Level | Our guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Numerical Reasoning | 10 min | Intermediate | [Numerical reasoning](sections/numerical-reasoning.md) |
| Verbal Reasoning | 10 min | Intermediate | [Verbal reasoning](sections/verbal-reasoning.md) |
| Critical Thinking | 12 min | Advanced | [Critical thinking](sections/critical-thinking.md) |
| Attention to Detail (Textual) | 12 min in the header, 10 min in the page text | Intermediate | [Attention to detail](sections/attention-to-detail.md) |
| Spatial Reasoning | 10 min | Intermediate | [Spatial reasoning](sections/spatial-reasoning.md) |
| Problem Solving | 9 min | Intermediate | [Problem solving](sections/problem-solving.md) |

The other cognitive tests TestGorilla lists are: RCI (Rapid Cognitive Index), Abstract Reasoning, Computational Thinking, Intermediate Math, Mechanical Reasoning, Attention to Detail (Visual), Basic Math Calculations, Basic Double-Digit Math, Basic Triple-Digit Math, Understanding Instructions and Reading Comprehension. The methods in this guide carry over to most of them: the numerical page covers the maths ones, the verbal and critical thinking pages cover reading comprehension and instructions, and the spatial page covers abstract and mechanical reasoning in part.

TestGorilla does not publish the number of questions in each test on its public test pages. When this guide gives a per-question time, it is our study guidance, not an official figure.

## What else may be in your assessment

Cognitive tests are only one part of the library. TestGorilla's library also has categories for language, personality and culture, programming, role-specific skills, situational judgment, software skills and typing speed. Employers can add other tools too:

- **Personality and culture tests.** TestGorilla lists six: Big 5 (OCEAN), DISC, Culture Add, 16 Personality Types, Motivation and Enneagram, most at 10 minutes. These describe how you tend to work rather than how much you know. Answer honestly and consistently. Trying to guess the "ideal" profile usually makes your answers less consistent, not more impressive.
- **Custom questions.** Written by the employer. TestGorilla's formats include essay, multiple choice, file upload (for a CV, portfolio or take-home task) and video.
- **Video questions.** You record an answer with your webcam. TestGorilla recommends recording a short test video during setup so you know your camera and microphone work.
- **AI interviews.** Open-ended interview questions, scored by AI against a structured rubric.
- **Qualifying questions.** Short screening questions before the main assessment, about the employer's minimum requirements. If an answer does not meet the employer's minimum, the assessment ends early.
- **Typing and coding tests,** for roles that need them.

## How TestGorilla protects the tests

TestGorilla describes these measures in its help centre. Some are always on, and some the employer can switch off:

- **Webcam snapshots** taken periodically, with automatic analysis that flags things like a second person or a covered camera. The employer can turn snapshots off.
- **Full-screen tracking.** Always on. If you leave full-screen, the current question is hidden until you return, and the timer keeps running.
- **Hidden question on screenshot attempts.** Always on. Pressing a screenshot shortcut, switching window or moving the mouse outside the page hides the question until you click to show it again.
- **Tab switching, developer tools, paste detection and approximate IP location** are logged.
- **Single-screen requirement.** On by default. If a second display is connected, you cannot start until it is unplugged or set to mirror mode.
- **Question banks.** TestGorilla says every test in its library has a bank numbering in the hundreds, questions are cycled, and each question is retired after a maximum number of uses.

TestGorilla is clear that these signals are not proof of cheating, and it tells employers not to reject a candidate on them alone. The practical point for you: stay in full-screen, keep your face in view, keep other windows closed, and use only pen, paper and a calculator. More on this in [the candidate experience](candidate-experience.md).

## The rules that shape your strategy

Four official rules matter more than any trick:

1. **You cannot go back.** Answer each question as if it is your only chance, because it is.
2. **Skipped means wrong, and wrong costs nothing extra.** Always choose an answer, even a guess.
3. **The timer keeps running** if you leave the window, so take breaks only between tests, when the platform offers one.
4. **Practice questions do not count.** Most tests start with four practice questions. Use them to learn the layout and the timer.

## Practise the real format

A TestGorilla assessment feels like several short sprints back to back. To rehearse that, take the [free TestGorilla-style practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/testgorilla?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=t7cs68&utm_content=testgorilla), which mixes numerical, verbal, critical thinking and attention to detail questions under a timer, then use the [5-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md) to find your weakest section.
