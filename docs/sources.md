## What TestGorilla does not publish

- The number of questions in each test. The public test pages give only the duration.
- A pass mark. Each employer sets its own threshold, weights and benchmark.
- The exact time for Attention to Detail (Textual): the page shows both 12 and 10 minutes.

## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not TestGorilla statements, and are labelled as guidance where they appear:

- the per-question timing budgets and clock checkpoints
- the "about 75% correct on practice" target
- the step-by-step methods, traps lists and study plans
- the examples of how each question type may look
- all practice items and explanations, which are original
