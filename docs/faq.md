# Frequently asked questions

These are the questions people search for most about TestGorilla, drawn from Google's "People also ask" results for US searches on "testgorilla test", "testgorilla assessment" and "testgorilla cheating" in November 2026, plus the questions TestGorilla's own candidate FAQ answers. Answers stick to what TestGorilla states, and say so when the answer is our guidance.

## What is a TestGorilla assessment?

An online hiring assessment that an employer builds on the TestGorilla platform. It contains up to five skills tests from TestGorilla's library, such as numerical reasoning or attention to detail, and can add the employer's own questions, video questions and other tools. Every test is timed. See [how a TestGorilla assessment works](test-overview.md).

## How hard is TestGorilla?

It depends on the tests your employer picked. TestGorilla gives each test a level: in the cognitive category, most are intermediate, a few basic maths and instruction tests are entry-level, and Critical Thinking is advanced. What makes most people find it hard is the time: each test lasts 9 to 13 minutes, you cannot go back, and the timer keeps running if you leave the window.

## How do I pass a TestGorilla assessment?

There is no fixed pass mark. The employer decides what score it wants, often using percentiles that compare you with other candidates. What helps most:

1. Find out which tests are on your invitation and practise those question types.
2. Answer every question, because skipped questions count as wrong and wrong answers carry no extra penalty.
3. Use the four practice questions at the start of each test to learn the layout.
4. Set up properly: full-screen, one monitor, notifications off, pen, paper and a calculator ready.

The [study plans](study-plans.md) put this into a day-by-day schedule.

## How do I prepare for an online assessment?

Start with the format, then the content, then the conditions. Learn what each test asks (the [section guides](../README.md#the-six-tests-at-a-glance) cover the six most common cognitive tests), practise the question types under a timer, and rehearse your setup: camera, microphone, quiet room, one screen. Our guidance is that one or two full timed practice runs before the real thing make the biggest difference to pacing.

## How do I know if I passed the assessment test?

TestGorilla does not tell you that you passed or failed. It sends your results to the employer, and the employer decides what happens next. If the employer chooses to share your results, you can see them on your TestGorilla profile. For any news about your application, contact the employer: TestGorilla says it has no access to that information.

## Can you fail an assessment and still get hired?

Yes, it can happen, because there is no fixed pass mark and the assessment is only one part of a hiring decision. Employers can weight tests differently, compare you with other applicants, and look at your CV and interviews too. But a low score on a heavily weighted test makes it harder, so treat every test as if it counts.

## Does TestGorilla detect cheating?

TestGorilla monitors several signals: webcam snapshots (with automatic checks for things like another person in frame), full-screen exits, tab switches, second-screen reconnections, opened developer tools, pasted content and approximate IP location. It groups these into three tiers for the employer: No Issues, Some Issues and Major Issues. TestGorilla also cycles questions from large banks and blocks repeat attempts from the same email.

TestGorilla says these signals are not proof of cheating and tells employers not to reject candidates on them alone. Its candidate honesty policy bans AI tools, outside help, sharing test content and taking tests more than once for the same application.

## Does TestGorilla record your screen?

TestGorilla's help centre does not list screen recording among its anti-cheating measures. What it does describe is webcam snapshots, full-screen tracking, tab-switch tracking, paste detection and hiding the question if you press a screenshot shortcut or move the mouse outside the page. Video questions record you through your webcam, but only for the answer you are giving.

## Can I go back to a previous question?

No. TestGorilla shows one question per screen, and once you submit or skip, you cannot return. If you are unsure, TestGorilla's own advice is to guess.

## Can I use a calculator?

Yes. TestGorilla's candidate guide says you can use pen, paper and a calculator during the assessment, but not AI or other tools.

## Can I retake a TestGorilla test?

Not on your own. Your invitation link works once. If you are worried about how it went, TestGorilla says to contact the employer, and any second attempt is at their discretion. TestGorilla also blocks repeat attempts from the same email address.

## How long does a TestGorilla assessment take?

TestGorilla says most assessments take about 60 to 90 minutes, but each employer builds its own. Your welcome screen shows the expected time before you begin. Individual cognitive tests run 9 to 13 minutes each.

## Is TestGorilla timed?

Yes. Every test has a timer, shown per test and/or per question. It starts when the test starts and keeps running even if you close the window. See the [timing guide](timing.md).

## Can I take a break?

Yes, between tests, when the platform offers a break. Once a test has started you must finish it. You return through the link in your invitation.

## Can I take it on my phone?

You can, but TestGorilla does not recommend it. It suggests a laptop or desktop computer and the Chrome browser, because small screens make questions hard to answer.

## Will I see my score?

Only if the employer chooses to share it. If they do, it appears on your TestGorilla profile. TestGorilla does not show the questions or correct answers to anyone.

## What languages are available?

TestGorilla offers the candidate experience in 12 languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, Dutch, Italian, Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese. You can only take the assessment in the language the employer chose. Not every test is available in every language: TestGorilla says its full library is in English and 40 or more tests are offered in each of the other languages.

## Can I practise on TestGorilla itself?

TestGorilla does not offer practice versions of employer assessments, and it blocks personal and student email addresses from signing up as customers. It does let candidates with a TestGorilla profile take individual skills tests and show the results to recruiters. TestGorilla's help page on this describes a limit of three tests every 24 hours and talks about retakes after three months, but parts of the page say retakes are not yet available, so check the current rules there. Those results are real and stay visible on your profile, so treat them as the real thing, not practice. For low-stakes practice, use original practice material such as the items in this guide or the [free TestGorilla-style practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/testgorilla?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=t7cs68&utm_content=testgorilla).
