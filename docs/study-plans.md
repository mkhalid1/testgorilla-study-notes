# Study plans

Two plans: one for a week, one for when the invitation is already in your inbox. Both start the same way: **open your invitation email and note which tests are listed.** TestGorilla's invitation includes a breakdown of the assessment's contents and how long you will have. There is no point drilling spatial reasoning if your assessment has no spatial test.

Then take the [15-minute diagnostic](diagnostic.md) for the sections you will face. Expect about 45 to 90 minutes a day on the 7-day plan.

## 7-day plan

| Day | Focus | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baseline | Read [how a TestGorilla assessment works](test-overview.md) and [the candidate experience](candidate-experience.md). Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md). Note your two weakest sections among the tests on your invitation. |
| 2 | Weakest section | Read its guide in full. Do all 12 items, untimed. For each miss, write one line: what you chose, what was right, and the rule you broke. |
| 3 | Second weakest section | Same as day 2. Then redo yesterday's misses without looking at the answers. |
| 4 | Remaining sections | Read the "How the questions look" and "Traps" parts of every other section on your invitation, and do their items timed. |
| 5 | First timed run | Take a full timed practice test, such as the [free TestGorilla-style one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/testgorilla?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=testgorilla). Score it by section. Read the [timing guide](timing.md). |
| 6 | Fix and rehearse | Go back to the traps list for whatever cost you most on day 5. If your assessment includes a video question, record a two-minute practice answer on your laptop camera and watch it back. |
| 7 | Light review and setup | Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Check your camera, microphone and browser. Do 10 mixed items, then stop. Sit the real assessment when you are fresh. |

### How to review a missed item

For each miss, answer three questions:

1. **Which rule did I break?** For example: reversed an "all" statement, took a percentage of the wrong base, chose a mirror image.
2. **What on the screen should have stopped me?** Name the word, digit or clue.
3. **What will I check next time?** One short habit, such as "write the setup line before calculating".

Keep these in one list. By day 6 the same two or three mistakes will keep coming back. Fixing those is worth more than any number of new questions.

## 2-day plan

For when the link has arrived and the deadline is close. Employers can set a deadline, and TestGorilla sends a reminder within 48 hours if you have not started. Check your invitation for the date.

### Day 1: learn the formats

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 min | Read your invitation. List the tests and the total time. |
| 2 | 15 min | Take the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md), only the parts that match your tests. |
| 3 | 25 min | Read the "How the questions look" and "Traps" parts of each section guide on your list. |
| 4 | 40 min | Do the practice items for your two weakest sections. Read every explanation, including the ones you got right. |
| 5 | 20 min | Take a full timed practice test, such as the [free TestGorilla-style one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/testgorilla?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=testgorilla). |

### Day 2: set up and sit it

| Block | Time | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 min | Redo yesterday's misses without the answers. |
| 2 | 10 min | Read the [test-day checklist](test-day-checklist.md). Unplug any second screen and close other apps. |
| 3 | 10 min | Warm up with a few mixed items you have not seen. |
| 4 | Assessment | Sit it in a quiet room at a time when you are usually sharp, with pen, paper and a calculator on the desk. |

If you have only one evening, do day 1 blocks 1, 3 and 5, then sit the assessment the next morning.

## What not to do

- **Do not hunt for "real" TestGorilla questions.** TestGorilla says each test draws from a bank of hundreds of questions, cycles them, and retires them after a set number of uses. Leaked lists will not match yours, and sharing test content breaks TestGorilla's candidate honesty policy.
- **Do not plan to retake.** Your invitation link works once. A second attempt is entirely up to the employer.
- **Do not use AI or another person for help.** It breaks the honesty agreement you sign at the start, and the anti-cheating signals are shown to the employer.
- **Do not split the assessment across a busy day.** You can take breaks between tests, but each test must be finished in one go once started.
