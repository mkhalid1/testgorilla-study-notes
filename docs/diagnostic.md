# 15-minute diagnostic

Eighteen original items, three for each of the six cognitive tests in this guide, to show you where to start. Set a timer for 15 minutes. Use pen, paper and a calculator if you like, since TestGorilla allows them. Answer every item, then check your answers and read the table at the bottom.

These items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from TestGorilla or any publisher's materials.

If you already know the question types, skip this and take the [free timed TestGorilla-style practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/testgorilla?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=testgorilla) instead. A full timed run gives a better picture of your pacing.

## Part A: Numerical reasoning

**D1.** What is 25% of 84?

- A. 19
- B. 21
- C. 24
- D. 28

**D2.** What number comes next? 2, 6, 18, 54, ?

- A. 108
- B. 72
- C. 162
- D. 216

**D3.** A team has finished 45 of its 60 tasks. What percentage of the tasks are still left?

- A. 15%
- B. 25%
- C. 33%
- D. 75%

## Part B: Verbal reasoning

**D4.** COLD is to FREEZE as HOT is to:

- A. warm
- B. boil
- C. ice
- D. summer

**D5.** Which word is closest in meaning to CANDID?

- A. frank
- B. sweet
- C. careful
- D. secret

**D6.** All of the interns were given laptops. Some people with laptops work remotely. Which statement must be true?

- A. Some interns work remotely.
- B. All remote workers are interns.
- C. No interns work remotely.
- D. Every intern has a laptop.

## Part C: Critical thinking

**D7.** If the report is late, the client will complain. The client did not complain. Which statement must be true?

- A. The report was early.
- B. The report was not late.
- C. The client did not read the report.
- D. The report will be late next time.

**D8.** "Everyone I have asked likes the new logo, so our customers will like it too." What is the main flaw?

- A. It assumes the people asked represent the customers.
- B. It attacks the people who dislike the logo.
- C. It uses its conclusion as its reason.
- D. It confuses cause and effect.

**D9.** Four runners finished a race. Kim finished before Lee. Max finished last. Nia finished immediately after Kim. Who finished third?

- A. Kim
- B. Lee
- C. Nia
- D. Max

## Part D: Attention to detail

**D10.** Which option matches `RX-77031-B` exactly?

- A. `RX-77301-B`
- B. `RX-77031-B`
- C. `RX-7703l-B`
- D. `RX-77031-8`

**D11.** How many of these pairs are exactly the same?

```
1.  Kowalski, Anna     Kowalski, Anna
2.  44 7700 900 312    44 7700 900 321
3.  Order #A-5509      Order #A-5509
```

- A. 0
- B. 1
- C. 2
- D. 3

**D12.** A rule says "All invoice numbers start with INV- followed by five digits." Which one breaks the rule?

- A. INV-30418
- B. INV-77102
- C. INV-0592
- D. INV-11846

## Part E: Spatial reasoning

**D13.** You face east. You turn left, turn left again, then turn right. Which way do you face now?

- A. North
- B. South
- C. East
- D. West

**D14.** A 3 by 3 by 3 cube is painted on every outside face, then cut into 27 equal small cubes. How many small cubes have exactly one painted face?

- A. 1
- B. 6
- C. 8
- D. 12

**D15.** A square sheet is folded in half so the top half lies on the bottom half. A hole is punched through the bottom-left corner of the folded paper. Where are the holes when the sheet is opened?

- A. Bottom-left and bottom-right corners
- B. Bottom-left corner only
- C. Top-left and bottom-left corners
- D. Top-right and bottom-left corners

## Part F: Problem solving

**D16.** Three calls, with Ana, Raj and Sue, must fill the 1:00, 2:00 and 3:00 slots, one per slot. Raj cannot take the 1:00 slot. Sue must be after Raj. Who is at 1:00?

- A. Ana
- B. Raj
- C. Sue
- D. It cannot be worked out

**D17.** Task A takes 2 days. Task B takes 3 days and can start only when A is done. Task C takes 4 days and can start at the same time as A. Task D takes 1 day and needs both B and C done. What is the shortest time for the whole job?

- A. 5 days
- B. 6 days
- C. 7 days
- D. 10 days

**D18.** Orders are packed by these rules: express orders first, then the order with the most items, then the oldest. Order 1: standard, 6 items. Order 2: express, 2 items. Order 3: standard, 9 items. Which order is packed second?

- A. Order 1
- B. Order 2
- C. Order 3
- D. It cannot be worked out

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations, D1 to D18</summary>

**D1: B (21).** 84 ÷ 4 = 21.

**D2: C (162).** Each number is three times the one before. 54 × 3 = 162. *Why not B:* 72 adds 18 instead of multiplying.

**D3: B (25%).** 60 − 45 = 15 tasks left. 15 ÷ 60 = 25%. *Why not D:* 75% is the share finished.

**D4: B (boil).** Freezing is what happens when something gets cold enough. Boiling is what happens when something gets hot enough. *Why not A:* warm is a milder degree of hot, not the result.

**D5: A (frank).** Candid means open and honest. *Why not D:* secret is nearly the opposite.

**D6: D.** All interns were given laptops, so every intern has a laptop. *Why not A:* the people with laptops who work remotely might all be non-interns.

**D7: B.** "If late, then complaint." No complaint, so not late. *Why not A:* not late could mean on time, not necessarily early.

**D8: A.** The people asked are a small group the speaker knows, who may not be like the customers. The argument generalises from them anyway.

**D9: B (Lee).** Max is fourth. Kim comes before Lee, and Nia comes straight after Kim. If Kim were second, Nia would be third and Lee would have to be first, before Kim, which breaks a clue. So Kim is first, and the only order that fits is Kim, Nia, Lee, Max. Lee is third. *Why not C:* Nia is second, straight after Kim.

**D10: B.** A swaps two digits (301). C has a lowercase l in place of the 1. D has an 8 in place of the B.

**D11: C (2).** Pairs 1 and 3 match. Pair 2 swaps the last two digits: 312 and 321.

**D12: C.** INV-0592 has only four digits after the hyphen.

**D13: A (north).** East, turn left: north. Turn left: west. Turn right: north.

**D14: B (6).** Only the centre square of each face has exactly one painted face. A cube has 6 faces.

**D15: C.** The folded corner has two layers: the bottom half's own bottom-left corner, and the top half folded down, whose matching point is the top-left corner. Opened, there are holes at the top-left and bottom-left.

**D16: A (Ana).** Raj cannot be at 1:00, and Sue must be after Raj, so Sue cannot be at 1:00 either. Ana is at 1:00, Raj at 2:00 and Sue at 3:00.

**D17: B (6 days).** A then B takes 2 + 3 = 5 days. C runs alongside and takes 4 days. D starts after day 5 and takes 1 day: 6 days in total.

**D18: C (Order 3).** Express first: Order 2. Then the most items: Order 3, with 9, beats Order 1, with 6.

</details>
