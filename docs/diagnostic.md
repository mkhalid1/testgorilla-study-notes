# 5-minute diagnostic

Six original items, one for each of the six cognitive tests in this guide, to show you where to start. Set a timer for 5 minutes. Use pen, paper and a calculator if you like, since TestGorilla allows them. Answer every item, then check your answers and read the table at the bottom.

These items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from TestGorilla or any publisher's materials.

If you already know the question types, skip this and take the [free timed TestGorilla-style practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/testgorilla?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=testgorilla) instead. A full timed run gives a better picture of your pacing.

## Part A: Numerical reasoning

**D1.** A team has finished 45 of its 60 tasks. What percentage of the tasks are still left?

- A. 15%
- B. 25%
- C. 33%
- D. 75%

## Part B: Verbal reasoning

**D2.** COLD is to FREEZE as HOT is to:

- A. warm
- B. boil
- C. ice
- D. summer

## Part C: Critical thinking

**D3.** "Everyone I have asked likes the new logo, so our customers will like it too." What is the main flaw?

- A. It assumes the people asked represent the customers.
- B. It attacks the people who dislike the logo.
- C. It uses its conclusion as its reason.
- D. It confuses cause and effect.

## Part D: Attention to detail

**D4.** A rule says "All invoice numbers start with INV- followed by five digits." Which one breaks the rule?

- A. INV-30418
- B. INV-77102
- C. INV-0592
- D. INV-11846

## Part E: Spatial reasoning

**D5.** A 3 by 3 by 3 cube is painted on every outside face, then cut into 27 equal small cubes. How many small cubes have exactly one painted face?

- A. 1
- B. 6
- C. 8
- D. 12

## Part F: Problem solving

**D6.** Task A takes 2 days. Task B takes 3 days and can start only when A is done. Task C takes 4 days and can start at the same time as A. Task D takes 1 day and needs both B and C done. What is the shortest time for the whole job?

- A. 5 days
- B. 6 days
- C. 7 days
- D. 10 days

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations, D1 to D6</summary>

**D1: B (25%).** 60 − 45 = 15 tasks left. 15 ÷ 60 = 25%. *Why not D:* 75% is the share finished.

**D2: B (boil).** Freezing is what happens when something gets cold enough. Boiling is what happens when something gets hot enough. *Why not A:* warm is a milder degree of hot, not the result.

**D3: A.** The people asked are a small group the speaker knows, who may not be like the customers. The argument generalises from them anyway.

**D4: C.** INV-0592 has only four digits after the hyphen.

**D5: B (6).** Only the centre square of each face has exactly one painted face. A cube has 6 faces.

**D6: B (6 days).** A then B takes 2 + 3 = 5 days. C runs alongside and takes 4 days. D starts after day 5 and takes 1 day: 6 days in total.

</details>
