# Test-day checklist

Print this or keep it open on your phone, then put the phone away before you start.

## The day before

- [ ] Reread your invitation: the tests listed, the total time and any deadline.
- [ ] Decide when you will sit it: a quiet slot of about 60 to 90 minutes, when you are usually sharp. TestGorilla says most assessments take about that long, and your welcome screen gives the real estimate.
- [ ] Use a laptop or desktop with Chrome. TestGorilla does not recommend a phone, because the small screen makes questions hard to answer.
- [ ] Check your camera, microphone and internet connection.
- [ ] Skim the "Traps" lists for the tests on your invitation.

## One hour before

- [ ] Unplug any second monitor, TV or docking-station screen, or set it to mirror mode.
- [ ] Close every other app and browser tab. Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notification pulls you out of full-screen.
- [ ] Put pen, scrap paper and a calculator on the desk. TestGorilla allows all three.
- [ ] Sit in good light with a plain background, alone in the room.
- [ ] Get water, use the bathroom. Breaks are only possible between tests.

## In the first minutes

- [ ] Read the welcome screen: number of tests, number of questions, expected time.
- [ ] Answer the accommodation questions honestly if they appear.
- [ ] Record the short test video when the camera setup screen offers it.
- [ ] Use the four practice questions at the start of each test to learn the layout. They do not count.

## During each test

- [ ] One question at a time. Submit, then forget it. You cannot go back.
- [ ] Never leave a question blank. Skipped counts as wrong, and a wrong guess costs nothing extra.
- [ ] Watch the timer, but do not stare at it. Check it about every few questions.
- [ ] Submit the question in front of you before time runs out. An unsubmitted answer is not saved.
- [ ] Stay in full-screen. If you leave by accident, go straight back.

## Between tests

- [ ] Take the break if you need it. Stand up, drink water, then come back through the invitation link if you closed the window.
- [ ] Do not dwell on the last test. Each test is scored on its own.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Look out for the email "Your assessment was submitted, what's next?".
- [ ] Create a TestGorilla profile if you want to see your results, in case the employer shares them.
- [ ] If something went wrong technically, contact the employer. Retakes are their decision.

## Last practice before the real one

If you have a spare 20 minutes the day before, take the [free TestGorilla-style practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/testgorilla?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=testgorilla) as a final timed warm-up, then stop studying for the day.
