# Glossary

Terms used in these notes and on the TestGorilla platform.

**Accommodation.** An adjustment, such as extra time, that a candidate can request at the start of an assessment if the employer has turned the option on.

**Analogy.** A word question of the form "A is to B as C is to ?", where the answer has the same relationship to C as B has to A.

**Assessment.** The whole package an employer sends you on TestGorilla: up to five tests plus any custom questions.

**Behaviour tier.** TestGorilla's grouping of a candidate's anti-cheating signals into No Issues, Some Issues or Major Issues.

**Benchmark.** The group your score is compared with. TestGorilla's default is All Candidates who took the same test.

**Contrapositive.** The one safe reversal of an "if" rule. From "if P then Q" you can conclude "if not Q then not P".

**Custom question.** A question the employer writes, in formats such as essay, multiple choice, file upload or video.

**Data interpretation.** A numerical question based on a table, chart or graph.

**Denial test.** A way to check an assumption: flip it to its opposite. If the argument falls apart, the argument relies on that assumption.

**Fallacy.** A flaw in reasoning, such as assuming that one event caused another just because it came first.

**Full-screen tracking.** TestGorilla's check that you stay in the full-screen test window. Leaving hides the current question until you return.

**Honesty agreement.** The statement you agree to before starting, confirming you will not use AI tools, outside help or repeat attempts.

**Mental folding.** Picturing what a flat shape looks like when folded, or a folded sheet when opened.

**Mental rotation.** Picturing a shape after it has been turned, without flipping it.

**Net (of a cube).** A flat pattern of six squares that folds up into a cube.

**Percentile.** The share of the comparison group you scored better than. A 70th percentile beats 70% of that group. Not the same as 70% correct.

**Practice questions.** The questions at the start of most tests, usually four, that show the format and do not count toward your score.

**Qualifying questions.** Screening questions an employer can put before the assessment. Failing one ends the assessment early.

**Question bank.** The large pool a test's questions are drawn from. TestGorilla says each test's bank numbers in the hundreds.

**Raw score.** The percentage of a test's questions you answered correctly. Also called % correct.

**Syllogism.** An argument with two or more "all", "some" or "no" statements, followed by a conclusion that may or may not follow.

**Test.** One set of questions on one skill, such as Numerical Reasoning, drawn from TestGorilla's question bank.

**Weighted score.** A combined score across the tests in an assessment, where the employer makes some tests count more than others.
